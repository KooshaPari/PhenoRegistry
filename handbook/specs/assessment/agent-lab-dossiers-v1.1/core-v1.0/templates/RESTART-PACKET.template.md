# Fresh-session packet — unaccepted template

**Mandate and limits:** actual source, allowed actions, reserved decisions, cumulative remaining resources, expiration/stop conditions.

**Current assignment:** epoch; intent and scope; required gates; criterion/evaluator/fixture/environment versions; accepted exclusions and unresolved applicability.

**State:** subject source/tree/dirty/BOM/artifact identities; report revision; actual or pending registry receipt; active claim and worktree; last accepted baseline; useful unmerged candidate.

**Negative knowledge:** failure fingerprint; exact reproduction; hypotheses tried; observed outcomes and evidence; why each approach was rejected; conditions that would justify retrying.

**Next action:** one discriminating step, prerequisites, expected observation, acceptance/rejection/stop rule; alternatives and why this step separates them.

**Do not do:** overwrite other work, reset cumulative budget, infer authority from prose, repeat an unchanged failed strategy, or treat this summary as a replacement for evidence.
