# First real assessment without central services

## Inputs

One authorized subject root, actual standing permissions and available local tools. No fresh platform installation is required. Read existing audit and authority records first.

## Procedure

Confirm package integrity and self-tests. Resolve the subject's complete current tree/BOM/configuration; metadata-only `inspect` is a discovery aid, not a content snapshot. Keep dirty work distinct from committed state. Recover or derive intent and a single beneficiary outcome. Create unaccepted records in a new scratch location, then map them to the existing owner only when authorized.

Choose enough catalog criteria to assess that outcome and its relevant failure obligations. Resolve applicability and important measurement gaps. Bind real commands/procedures and qualify a positive and false-success witness. Lock the assignment. Collect real evidence; summarize consistently, but do not treat consistency as authenticity. Perform a permitted repair/experiment and verify separately. Checkpoint the accepted/candidate state and queue optional registry records.

## Completion

One meaningful measured result or learning outcome; honest uncertainty; a next agent can reproduce the important state. A written report alone is not the complete operational loop. Absence of a qualified measurement is a visible blocker for that claim, not an invented fail or pass.
