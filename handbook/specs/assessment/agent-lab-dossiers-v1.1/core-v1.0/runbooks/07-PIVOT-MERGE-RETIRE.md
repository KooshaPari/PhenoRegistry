# Pivot, merge or retire under a mandate

Separate quality findings from value evidence. A broken useful product suggests repair; a technically excellent unused capability may suggest an experiment, scope reduction or maintenance. Compare alternatives and no action. Use predeclared bounded observation windows and reversal conditions rather than oscillating after noisy scores.

Before a consequential boundary/lifecycle change, inventory actual consumers, contracts, data custody, secret ownership, active work, releases, migration and rollback/revival path. Check real authority for allocation, archival, publication and deletion separately. An agent-originated repository needs no ceremonial human justification, but its existence is not proof that its boundary is good.

Prefer reversible steps when value or dependencies are uncertain. Suspending new work is distinct from deleting artifacts. Preserve accepted evidence and historical rationale. State the experiment that could refute the recommendation, expected resource effect, affected beneficiaries, next owner, and wakeup/revival condition. A score is input, not the authority for the action.
