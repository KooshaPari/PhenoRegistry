# D15 — Data stewardship

Status: authored candidate predicates. No product results or automatically qualified adapters are implied. Exact-duplicate checks do not establish semantic uniqueness; resolve cross-cutting overlaps when weighting an assignment.

## D15-P01 — Data model and integrity
Applicability trigger: subjects storing or transforming durable data.
Method: **DYNAMIC**. Create representative valid and invalid records and execute supported transformations; compare against independently stated integrity predicates.

- **PEP-15-01-01** — Required fields cannot be silently omitted from accepted records.
- **PEP-15-01-02** — Identifiers preserve their declared uniqueness scope.
- **PEP-15-01-03** — Referential relationships remain valid across supported mutations.
- **PEP-15-01-04** — Units and types remain consistent during data conversion.
- **PEP-15-01-05** — Invalid state transitions are rejected.
- **PEP-15-01-06** — Concurrent updates preserve the declared conflict semantics.
- **PEP-15-01-07** — A failed transaction does not leave a forbidden partial state.
- **PEP-15-01-08** — Round-trip serialization preserves required information.
- **PEP-15-01-09** — Derived projections can be traced to authoritative records.
- **PEP-15-01-10** — Integrity validation detects a deliberately corrupted fixture.

## D15-P02 — Migrations and compatibility
Applicability trigger: stateful products with schema or storage evolution.
Method: **DYNAMIC**. Run supported upgrade and rollback sequences on representative old datasets; inspect preserved information, locks and partial failures.

- **PEP-15-02-01** — A supported old dataset can be upgraded to the selected version.
- **PEP-15-02-02** — Migration preserves required user information.
- **PEP-15-02-03** — Migration records identify the source and target schema versions.
- **PEP-15-02-04** — An interrupted migration has a supported continuation or rollback path.
- **PEP-15-02-05** — Rollback limitations are explicit before the upgrade is accepted.
- **PEP-15-02-06** — Concurrent access during migration follows the declared availability contract.
- **PEP-15-02-07** — A migration cannot silently operate on the wrong environment.
- **PEP-15-02-08** — Large-data migration behavior is evaluated under a representative volume.
- **PEP-15-02-09** — Incompatible older consumers fail predictably after an unsupported transition.
- **PEP-15-02-10** — A no-op migration is not reported as proof of data transformation correctness.

## D15-P03 — Privacy and data minimization
Applicability trigger: subjects handling personal, private or restricted data.
Method: **SEMANTIC**. Map actual data flows against purposes and authorized boundaries; validate the selected minimization and redaction behaviors with synthetic records.

- **PEP-15-03-01** — Collected sensitive fields have a stated product purpose.
- **PEP-15-03-02** — Unnecessary sensitive data is not collected by default.
- **PEP-15-03-03** — Data flows identify external processors or destinations.
- **PEP-15-03-04** — Private research or source content is not published through a generated index accidentally.
- **PEP-15-03-05** — Access to raw evidence follows the sensitivity of its contents.
- **PEP-15-03-06** — Telemetry collection distinguishes required operations from optional analytics.
- **PEP-15-03-07** — Derived embeddings or caches are included in the data-flow review when relevant.
- **PEP-15-03-08** — A public demo uses authorized synthetic or appropriately redacted data.
- **PEP-15-03-09** — Privacy claims identify their scope and supporting observations.
- **PEP-15-03-10** — Unresolved legal or consent obligations remain explicit rather than inferred from a technical pass.

## D15-P04 — Retention, deletion and export
Applicability trigger: products retaining user or operational data.
Method: **DYNAMIC**. Exercise retention expiry, scoped deletion and authorized export using representative linked records and derived stores.

- **PEP-15-04-01** — Retention periods are defined for the relevant data classes.
- **PEP-15-04-02** — Expired data is removed or retained under an explicit exception.
- **PEP-15-04-03** — Deletion targets the authorized subject rather than unrelated records.
- **PEP-15-04-04** — Required deletion propagates to relevant derived stores.
- **PEP-15-04-05** — Backup retention limitations are disclosed in deletion claims.
- **PEP-15-04-06** — Exports contain the promised data scope in a usable format.
- **PEP-15-04-07** — Export authorization prevents access to another principal's data.
- **PEP-15-04-08** — A deleted record is not recreated silently from a stale projection.
- **PEP-15-04-09** — Permitted integrity metadata can survive without retaining prohibited raw payloads.
- **PEP-15-04-10** — Deletion and export receipts identify their actual completed scope.

## D15-P05 — Lineage, synchronization and reconciliation
Applicability trigger: subjects with replicated, derived or synchronized data.
Method: **DYNAMIC**. Create controlled divergent updates and replay deliveries; verify identity, ordering, conflict handling and provenance.

- **PEP-15-05-01** — A derived record identifies the source version used.
- **PEP-15-05-02** — Duplicate delivery does not duplicate the logical object.
- **PEP-15-05-03** — Out-of-order events follow a declared reconciliation rule.
- **PEP-15-05-04** — Conflicting updates preserve enough information for resolution.
- **PEP-15-05-05** — A failed synchronization remains visibly pending or blocked.
- **PEP-15-05-06** — Local completion is distinguishable from remote registration.
- **PEP-15-05-07** — Projection rebuilding produces the expected state from accepted records.
- **PEP-15-05-08** — A stale source cannot silently overwrite a newer accepted state.
- **PEP-15-05-09** — Cross-device timestamps are not treated as total ordering without a justified clock model.
- **PEP-15-05-10** — Reconciliation preserves supersession and correction history.
