# D17 — Governance and evidence integrity

Status: authored candidate predicates. No product results or automatically qualified adapters are implied. Exact-duplicate checks do not establish semantic uniqueness; resolve cross-cutting overlaps when weighting an assignment.

## D17-P01 — Record identity and schema integrity
Applicability trigger: all protocol records.
Method: **STRUCTURAL**. Validate the actual record structure, identifiers and cross-references; perturb revisions, types and references to verify rejection.

- **PEP-17-01-01** — Records have stable unique identifiers within their declared namespace.
- **PEP-17-01-02** — Each record declares its schema version.
- **PEP-17-01-03** — Required fields cannot be omitted silently.
- **PEP-17-01-04** — Unknown record types are not accepted as known facts.
- **PEP-17-01-05** — References resolve to the intended record or remain explicit unresolved links.
- **PEP-17-01-06** — Malformed timestamps cannot masquerade as valid observation times.
- **PEP-17-01-07** — A record's content digest is computed over a documented representation.
- **PEP-17-01-08** — Duplicate identifiers with different contents produce a conflict.
- **PEP-17-01-09** — Extensions cannot silently change the meaning of required fields.
- **PEP-17-01-10** — Template records are distinguishable from accepted operational records.

## D17-P02 — Subject, evidence and report provenance
Applicability trigger: all assessments.
Method: **STRUCTURAL**. Resolve subject, report, artifact, evaluator and evidence identities; recompute local digests and reject incorrect bindings.

- **PEP-17-02-01** — The result identifies the subject snapshot actually measured.
- **PEP-17-02-02** — The report-storage revision is separate from the evaluated source revision.
- **PEP-17-02-03** — Registry receipt identity is separate from local report completion.
- **PEP-17-02-04** — Evidence bytes match their recorded digest when locally verifiable.
- **PEP-17-02-05** — The evaluator and fixture revisions are identifiable.
- **PEP-17-02-06** — Observation time is separate from report-production and registration time.
- **PEP-17-02-07** — Dirty-worktree evidence records material uncommitted contents distinctly.
- **PEP-17-02-08** — A previous report is not accepted as fresh product evidence by itself.
- **PEP-17-02-09** — A superseding result preserves the earlier result identity.
- **PEP-17-02-10** — A hash record alone is not represented as an independently trusted signature.

## D17-P03 — Scoring and maturity integrity
Applicability trigger: all scorecards and maturity reports.
Method: **STRUCTURAL**. Recompute totals from the frozen assignment and effective result set; inject unknowns, stale passes, exclusions and gate failures.

- **PEP-17-03-01** — Assessment coverage is reported separately from assessed pass rate.
- **PEP-17-03-02** — Unknown results do not count as verified passes.
- **PEP-17-03-03** — Stale evidence cannot satisfy a current gate without an accepted reuse justification.
- **PEP-17-03-04** — Unresolved applicability remains visible outside the settled denominator.
- **PEP-17-03-05** — A critical gate cannot be compensated by unrelated low-risk passes.
- **PEP-17-03-06** — A waiver remains distinguishable from a pass.
- **PEP-17-03-07** — Duplicate criteria cannot create extra score weight.
- **PEP-17-03-08** — Empty denominators yield undefined values rather than perfect scores.
- **PEP-17-03-09** — Maturity is scoped to a beneficiary slice and actual product baseline.
- **PEP-17-03-10** — Scores across changed epochs distinguish product, evidence, scope, evaluator and policy deltas.

## D17-P04 — Assignment epochs and self-amendment
Applicability trigger: agent-defined assignments and evaluators.
Method: **SEMANTIC**. Compare frozen and proposed baselines, qualify changed obligations and inspect amendment authority; preserve urgent-stop semantics.

- **PEP-17-04-01** — An execution epoch freezes the material assignment and acceptance meaning.
- **PEP-17-04-02** — The assignment has a valid and a meaningful invalid witness where feasible.
- **PEP-17-04-03** — Requirement contradictions have a visible refutation or resolution record.
- **PEP-17-04-04** — Changing a grader creates an attributable evaluator revision.
- **PEP-17-04-05** — Removing an obligation identifies the rationale and affected consumers.
- **PEP-17-04-06** — The builder cannot silently reduce an active acceptance threshold.
- **PEP-17-04-07** — A corrected oracle does not retroactively rewrite the old product verdict.
- **PEP-17-04-08** — An urgent safety or integrity defect can stop affected promotion before the next scheduled review.
- **PEP-17-04-09** — Self-amendment remains inside actual delegated policy authority.
- **PEP-17-04-10** — The bootstrap trust boundary is declared as an assumption rather than an absolute guarantee.

## D17-P05 — Ledger, outbox and concurrency integrity
Applicability trigger: records synchronized across workers or registries.
Method: **DYNAMIC**. Replay, duplicate, reorder and interrupt deliveries; verify idempotency, conflict detection and honest local-versus-remote state.

- **PEP-17-05-01** — Each delivery has an idempotency identity.
- **PEP-17-05-02** — Duplicate delivery does not create duplicate accepted events.
- **PEP-17-05-03** — Concurrent producers do not overwrite one shared mutable event record.
- **PEP-17-05-04** — A failed delivery remains queued or blocked with its actual reason.
- **PEP-17-05-05** — Receipt contents identify the payload actually accepted by the destination.
- **PEP-17-05-06** — An outbox item is not reported as a completed registry write.
- **PEP-17-05-07** — Correction events preserve the original history.
- **PEP-17-05-08** — Conflicting accepted projections require explicit reconciliation.
- **PEP-17-05-09** — A stale writer cannot overwrite a newer projection without detection.
- **PEP-17-05-10** — Registry unavailability does not prevent independent authorized local work.

## D17-P06 — Assurance boundaries and evaluator security
Applicability trigger: evaluation pipelines and autonomous decision paths.
Method: **DYNAMIC**. Inject forged records, untrusted instructions, altered fixtures and unauthorized effects; inspect actual enforcement and independent observations.

- **PEP-17-06-01** — Repository instructions cannot redefine the evaluator's actual permissions.
- **PEP-17-06-02** — A forged self-reported success cannot replace required raw evidence.
- **PEP-17-06-03** — Protected holdouts are inaccessible to unauthorized implementation workers.
- **PEP-17-06-04** — Verifier output is bound to the intended artifact.
- **PEP-17-06-05** — A compromised optional report renderer cannot change accepted measurements.
- **PEP-17-06-06** — Semantic reviewers have an explicit unknown or contested outcome.
- **PEP-17-06-07** — Independent verification claims identify the real separation mechanism.
- **PEP-17-06-08** — An unavailable required expert evaluation remains a visible limit on the claim.
- **PEP-17-06-09** — An evaluator update is tested against both known-good and known-bad cases.
- **PEP-17-06-10** — Qualification of one instrument is not generalized to untested adapters.
