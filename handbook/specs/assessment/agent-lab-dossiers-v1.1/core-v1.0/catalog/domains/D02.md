# D02 — Research and decision quality

Status: authored candidate predicates. No product results or automatically qualified adapters are implied. Exact-duplicate checks do not establish semantic uniqueness; resolve cross-cutting overlaps when weighting an assignment.

## D02-P01 — Source provenance and acquisition
Applicability trigger: subjects using research or external evidence.
Method: **STRUCTURAL**. Inspect source records against accessible originals; verify identity, retrieval scope, versions and rights without fabricating unavailable content.

- **PEP-02-01-01** — Each material research claim has an identifiable source record.
- **PEP-02-01-02** — Source records distinguish primary evidence from secondary interpretation.
- **PEP-02-01-03** — Retrieval timestamps are recorded separately from publication dates.
- **PEP-02-01-04** — Versioned software evidence identifies the inspected revision or release.
- **PEP-02-01-05** — Unavailable or access-blocked sources remain explicit acquisition gaps.
- **PEP-02-01-06** — Quoted passages preserve the meaning of their source context.
- **PEP-02-01-07** — Source acquisition preserves the original content digest when permitted.
- **PEP-02-01-08** — Mirrors and reposts are not counted as independent corroboration.
- **PEP-02-01-09** — Rights restrictions are recorded before redistributing source material.
- **PEP-02-01-10** — Search coverage limitations accompany claims of research completeness.

## D02-P02 — Claim analysis and contradictory evidence
Applicability trigger: subjects using research to justify decisions.
Method: **SEMANTIC**. Extract claims with source anchors; seek counterexamples and evaluate at least the serious alternatives supported by the decision context.

- **PEP-02-02-01** — Material claims are small enough to be independently supported or refuted.
- **PEP-02-02-02** — The cited evidence supports the exact claim rather than a related topic.
- **PEP-02-02-03** — Uncertainty in a source is preserved in the derived claim.
- **PEP-02-02-04** — Contradictory sources remain visible in the synthesis.
- **PEP-02-02-05** — Correlation is not silently promoted to a causal explanation.
- **PEP-02-02-06** — A source's experiment population is not silently broadened.
- **PEP-02-02-07** — A lack of evidence is not represented as evidence of absence.
- **PEP-02-02-08** — Author speculation is distinguished from reported observations.
- **PEP-02-02-09** — An alternative explanation accompanies high-impact causal claims.
- **PEP-02-02-10** — The decision can be reconstructed without reading an entire transcript.

## D02-P03 — Research freshness and source drift
Applicability trigger: subjects relying on time-sensitive external facts.
Method: **STRUCTURAL**. Compare current retrieval metadata with frozen source versions; record changed claims and invalidate only affected decisions.

- **PEP-02-03-01** — Time-sensitive sources have claim-specific review triggers.
- **PEP-02-03-02** — A changed source does not silently replace the historical source version.
- **PEP-02-03-03** — Broken links have a retained identifier or an explicit provenance failure.
- **PEP-02-03-04** — Framework-version changes identify affected local mappings.
- **PEP-02-03-05** — Current status claims do not rely solely on an old publication date.
- **PEP-02-03-06** — Source corrections propagate to dependent claims.
- **PEP-02-03-07** — An unchanged URL is not treated as proof of unchanged content.
- **PEP-02-03-08** — Research refresh records distinguish no change from failed retrieval.
- **PEP-02-03-09** — Expired evidence cannot satisfy a current time-sensitive gate without justification.
- **PEP-02-03-10** — Refresh effort is bounded by material impact rather than every source on every run.

## D02-P04 — Experiment design
Applicability trigger: subjects containing material untested assumptions.
Method: **SEMANTIC**. Construct an experiment with competing hypotheses, observable discriminators, controls, budget and an explicit interpretation table.

- **PEP-02-04-01** — The experiment states the uncertainty it is intended to resolve.
- **PEP-02-04-02** — Competing hypotheses predict distinguishable observations.
- **PEP-02-04-03** — The experimental unit is defined before data collection.
- **PEP-02-04-04** — Success and failure conditions are specified before examining results.
- **PEP-02-04-05** — Controls distinguish the proposed mechanism from a plausible confounder.
- **PEP-02-04-06** — The environment is representative of the claim being tested.
- **PEP-02-04-07** — Sample selection includes unsuccessful or incomplete attempts.
- **PEP-02-04-08** — The experiment has a finite resource and stopping rule.
- **PEP-02-04-09** — A negative result can change the planned next action.
- **PEP-02-04-10** — The planned measurement is sensitive enough to detect the relevant difference.

## D02-P05 — Analysis and reproducibility
Applicability trigger: subjects reporting measurements or experimental results.
Method: **DYNAMIC**. Recompute reported quantities from retained observations; repeat a bounded run and test a deliberately perturbed input.

- **PEP-02-05-01** — Reported summary statistics can be recomputed from retained observations.
- **PEP-02-05-02** — Units are consistent across inputs and outputs.
- **PEP-02-05-03** — Missing observations are disclosed rather than replaced with successful ones.
- **PEP-02-05-04** — Outlier exclusion follows a recorded rule.
- **PEP-02-05-05** — Uncertainty is reported where sampling variability is material.
- **PEP-02-05-06** — Censored or timed-out attempts are retained in analysis.
- **PEP-02-05-07** — Repeated observations are not falsely treated as independent when they share state.
- **PEP-02-05-08** — Comparisons use matched workload and environment conditions.
- **PEP-02-05-09** — Analysis code identifies the input dataset revision.
- **PEP-02-05-10** — A small deliberate data change produces the expected report change.

## D02-P06 — Research-to-action decisions
Applicability trigger: subjects making engineering or portfolio decisions from research.
Method: **SEMANTIC**. Trace each adopted idea to the actual problem, bounded experiment, consumer and rejected alternatives; permit justified no-op conclusions.

- **PEP-02-06-01** — The proposed adoption addresses a demonstrated or explicit hypothetical local problem.
- **PEP-02-06-02** — Analogy limits are stated before transferring a mechanism across domains.
- **PEP-02-06-03** — An implementation recommendation includes a smallest useful test.
- **PEP-02-06-04** — Research findings identify the repository that actually owns the concern.
- **PEP-02-06-05** — A dependency recommendation distinguishes feature donation from permanent adoption.
- **PEP-02-06-06** — Research that does not apply can terminate with a no-op decision.
- **PEP-02-06-07** — The expected benefit is compared with integration and maintenance cost.
- **PEP-02-06-08** — A decision identifies which assumption would reverse it.
- **PEP-02-06-09** — Research corpus size is not used as a substitute for decision quality.
- **PEP-02-06-10** — The chosen action has an observable post-adoption review condition.
