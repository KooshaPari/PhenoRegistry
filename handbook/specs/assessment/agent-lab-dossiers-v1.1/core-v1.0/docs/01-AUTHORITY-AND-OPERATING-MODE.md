# Authority and operating mode

## Mission

Operate a lab whose workers may be replaced without losing accepted state or negative knowledge. Workers may create products, repository roles, specifications and graders. Treat cheap-worker economics as a measurable hypothesis, not a license for unlimited process or retries. The protocol is an institutional memory and verification mechanism, not a product approval oracle.

## Three loops

**Mission/portfolio** selects outcomes and experiments. **Assignment/measurement** derives and qualifies the task, requirements and instruments. **Execution** implements and measures the frozen task. One harness can perform these as separate bounded passes; permanent managerial agents are optional. Passing an implementation test does not validate the mission. A technically legitimate experiment may remain value-unproven.

## Actual authority

Resolve existing operator instructions, provider restrictions, filesystem rights, repository ownership, credentials, resource limits and reserved decisions. The files in this ZIP grant none of those powers. An accepted local intent can be agent-derived or agent-originated; its authority comes from the parent mandate, not a forged sponsor quote.

An actor who may edit code cannot thereby edit accepted policy, publish a release, spend new funds or delete a repository. An actor who may perform those actions under a standing mandate does not need repeated ceremonial human approval. Agent reviewers and policy owners are valid roles when actually delegated.

Use `mandate`, `intent`, `repo-genesis`, `feedback` and `decision` records. Keep factual support separate from authority and commitment. `EXPERIMENT` means accepted to learn, not proven to deserve investment. A sponsor's preference can change the objective but cannot change a historical observation.

## Default effect modes

| Mode | Allowed output, subject to the real mandate |
|---|---|
| audit | Inventory, observations, findings, assessments; no product mutation implied |
| plan | Derived intent, experiments, assignments and work proposals |
| improve | Bounded implementation and verification in an authorized isolated work area |
| operate | Explicitly delegated operational actions with receipts and recovery |
| portfolio | Delegated allocation or lifecycle choices, including reversible experiments |

Missing rights block only the dependent effect. Missing historical intent creates a provenance finding, not a demand that the human invent a story. Missing central software leaves queued integration records, not a laboratory-wide stop.

## Escalation

Escalate when an action needs unavailable authority, a reserved decision, consequential ambiguous consent, or a form of external evidence that agents cannot legitimately manufacture. First distinguish a true authority boundary from low confidence that can be resolved by research or a safe bounded experiment. Record exact action, why existing authority is insufficient, alternatives, next action and wakeup condition. Do not ask the sponsor to restate already delegated routine authority.

## Dispositions

Continue, improve, experiment, research, narrow, merge, maintain, pause, pivot, retire and no action are all legitimate. Retirement needs consumer and custody analysis. Suspending allocation is not equivalent to deletion. Major decisions must include credible contrary explanations and a discriminating observation, not just a score.
