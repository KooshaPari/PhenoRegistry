# Restartable search and the three clocks

## Retain learning, replace workers

A checkpoint links mandate, intent, epoch, accepted baseline, current candidate branches, raw evidence, invalidated observations, failure signatures, rejected hypotheses, claim/lease state, remaining cumulative resources, next action and stop conditions. Summaries are navigation, not evidence. A new session gets no new privileges and no fresh unlimited budget.

Distinguish replay (same conditions for diagnosis), restart (new session context), reseed (different model, method, tool or decomposition), replan (new search strategy), and contract revision (changed target through a new epoch). Preserve exact changed factors so apparent improvement is interpretable.

## Stall diagnosis

Repeated equivalent patches suggest a strategy loop. Failures before product execution suggest an environment or runner fault. A good witness rejected or no-op accepted suggests an oracle defect. Mutually unsatisfiable obligations suggest a contract problem. Passing components with a broken real journey suggest a boundary defect. Strong engineering without beneficiary evidence suggests an intent/value question. Mismatched raw data and reports suggest evidence integrity failure.

Each diagnosis has a discriminating next step. Do not assume restarting fixes everything. Do not declare a problem impossible merely because several attempts failed. Bound the next experiment and retain its result even when no repair succeeds.

## Convergence statement

For a fixed success definition, conditional success probabilities p_k give P(no success through n attempts) = product(1-p_k). If p_k stays above a positive epsilon, the failure probability is bounded by (1-epsilon)^n. A fresh prompt, cheap worker or large agent count does not establish that bound. Wrong objectives, correlated assumptions, broken environments, weak oracles and changing targets can defeat the practical inference.

History can be append-only while beliefs and code quality regress. Preserve successful baselines and counterexamples, re-open invalidated claims, and count reopened defects. Distinguish finding one reusable valid artifact from reliably operating a stochastic agent on repeated requests [ANTHROPIC-EVALS].

## Three clocks

**Construction:** time to a functioning bounded slice through the actual dependency path. **Assurance:** delay until required evidence is current and adequate. **Outcome learning:** delay until the intended consumer benefit is supported or refuted. A repository can be advanced on one clock and early on the others.

Track net gate closure within an epoch, reopened-defect rate, time since last accepted outcome or useful learning, repeated failure fingerprints, blocked critical dependencies, evaluator disagreements and total cost per accepted result. Include setup, verification, tool/runtime spend, retries, integration and recovery. Cost estimates and observed charges must be separately labeled.

## Controlled comparison

Compare continued sessions, fresh-session restarts and varied strategies under matched tasks, total budgets and qualified frozen graders. Retain failed and censored tasks, environment/model versions and all verification costs. Use independent consumer or holdout outcomes where feasible. Do not select only wins. Policy thresholds for retries, cooldowns or reallocation are local experiments, not universal constants.
