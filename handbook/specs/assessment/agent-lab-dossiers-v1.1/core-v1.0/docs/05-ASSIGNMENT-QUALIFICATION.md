# Construct and qualify the assignment

Agents can construct the assignment, including agent-originated intent. The assignment must then be evaluated as a fallible artifact. A coherent specification and passing implementation can still be a self-consistent fiction.

## Assignment capsule

Bind mandate and intent revisions; beneficiary and outcome hypothesis; subject and product BOM; required and excluded behavior; criterion and evaluator revisions; measurement bindings; fixture and environment identity; evidence requirements; actual permitted effects; resource envelope; and stop/review conditions. Record where scope remains unresolved.

## Qualification questions

**Relevance:** Does satisfying the obligation help the parent outcome? Test the possibility that the proposed feature is unnecessary. **Consistency:** Can the requirements be satisfied together? Construct a concrete witness where feasible. **Discrimination:** Does the grader accept a valid non-identical solution and reject a meaningful defect? **Non-vacuity:** Did actual assertions run against actual behavior? **Scope:** Are mandatory journey, failure and recovery cases covered? **Grounding:** Does evidence reach an actual consumer, runtime effect, justified invariant or clearly labeled experiment? **Independence:** Can the builder silently edit the only evidence or oracle used to accept the result?

## Positive and negative controls

A valid witness demonstrates that the evaluator is not simply impossible or overly tied to one implementation. A negative witness demonstrates that plausible false success cannot pass. Useful mutations include no-op implementations, skipped assertions, a forced zero exit code, a mismatched artifact, wrong source SHA, a fake UI success state, stale data and mocked-only integration.

Keep test definitions and expected behavior tied to intent or justified properties, not merely copied from current code. Differential and metamorphic approaches can help, but their references and relations need their own justification. Semantic review uses paired anchors and an UNKNOWN route; agreement between two models is not an automatic gold standard.

## Epoch changes

Within an epoch, freeze acceptance meaning. When a requirement or grader is wrong, propose a new epoch with rationale, alternatives, changed obligations, qualification results and affected consumers. Reassess preserved subject snapshots under old and new baselines where useful. Classify movement as product, evidence, scope, evaluator or policy change. Do not claim that weakening a threshold improved code.

Urgent security, integrity or custody discoveries may stop the affected promotion immediately. Freezing an epoch is not permission to ignore new hazards. Agents can adjudicate normal amendments when delegated; they cannot grant themselves absent host privileges.

## Qualification depth

Use consequence and reversibility to select effort. A metadata parser needs boundary fixtures; a destructive migration needs real integrity/recovery experiments and strong enforcement. Preserve accepted mandatory floors. A prototype may expose an unresolved instrument and continue independent work, but cannot claim the gate the missing instrument was supposed to verify.
