## Comparison discipline

Three different comparisons are required and must not be conflated:

1. **Historical fork delta** — what owned work changed relative to the base at the time.
2. **Contemporary upstream delta** — what the active upstream now solves, changed, removed or made obsolete.
3. **Mature-contract fit** — whether either contemporary base can satisfy recovered accepted obligations plus the generic shared-harness architecture.

A capability disappearing from an owned fork but appearing in current upstream may be commodity/superseded rather than lost. A capability present only in an old owned fork may be accepted intent accidentally dropped, obsolete design, or genuine retained differentiation; provenance decides.

HeliosLite/Forgecode is a sunset donor and does not receive a third contemporary-primary baseline.
