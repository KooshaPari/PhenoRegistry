# Non-code/specification completion gate

A product may be marked NON_CODE_SPEC_FINAL only when:
- all recoverable intent/lineage sources are resolved or explicitly exhausted;
- contemporary upstream/SOTA research is dated and versioned;
- realistic alternative stack and differentiation ledger are complete;
- ontology and mature product horizon are accepted;
- semantic obligations are fully decomposed with no target count;
- quality overlays and journeys are complete;
- stage projections preserve mature spine;
- architecture decisions and unresolved experimental decisions are separated;
- bidirectional trace structure has no unexplained orphans;
- evidence identity/authority/grader policy is complete;
- positive/negative/adversarial oracle design is complete;
- source implementation mapping is complete as a mapping, even when implementation is absent;
- contradictions are resolved or remain explicit blocking decisions;
- registry/repo-local truth agrees;
- fresh independent review attempts to falsify completeness.

NON_CODE_SPEC_FINAL does **not** mean product complete. Runtime/code experiments, performance measurements, installable artifacts and empirical pilots remain separate gates and may block architecture decisions from becoming accepted.
