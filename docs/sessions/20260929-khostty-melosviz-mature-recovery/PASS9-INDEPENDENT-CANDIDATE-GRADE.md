## SOTA architecture implication

OpenTimelineIO remains a strong candidate for Melosviz's **editorial projection**, not its complete product state. Current official OTIO docs define Clip media references, rational time ranges, tracks/transitions/markers/metadata and adapter-based interchange; the canonical OTIO JSON serialization is lossless for the OTIO schema while other adapters may be lossy. Media itself remains externally referenced. Therefore Melosviz should not invent another generic editorial EDL/timeline interchange, but must retain product-specific audio-analysis, render-job, scene-intent, provenance and acceptance state outside or in explicit OTIO metadata/schema projections.

OpenUSD remains the candidate hybrid spatial projection because its composition model supplies layers/references/variants/overrides. Neither OTIO nor USD supplies Melosviz's durable render-attempt/evidence acceptance loop. The M-E02 repair should stay thin and not become a new generic workflow engine.
