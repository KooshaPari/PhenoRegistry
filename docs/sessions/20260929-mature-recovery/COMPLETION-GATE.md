## Evidence and progress doctrine

Distinguish accepted intent/design, deterministic source fact, verified observation, imported assertion, agent inference, experiment, proposal and superseded history. Confidence never upgrades authority. An acceptance record binds product/subject, contract/criterion, candidate/configuration/environment, verifier/version, run/time and raw artifact. Missing, skipped, unavailable, stale and wrong-candidate evidence is not green. A schema-valid envelope does not certify truth.

Track functional, traceability, evidence, journey closure, regression, performance, reliability, security, accessibility, usability, uncertainty and transition debt separately where applicable. No denominator has been accepted, so percentages remain unavailable. Contract/scope changes and engineering progress on an unchanged contract have separate deltas. Velocity, confidence and evidence age need observations; no asymptote is inferred.

The three lifetime model—worker attempt, durable development effort and product state—is common policy, not mechanically cloned per-feature requirements. A dying worker cannot erase accepted product truth or durable effort. Work completed is not product accepted.
