## Differentiation attack and existence decision

Commodity/contested: generic traces, dashboards, dataset comparisons and prompt optimization. Candidate differentiation: making owned heterogeneous agent-system changes scientifically interpretable and operationally safe across component/parent boundaries. Unverified: that this integration is superior to a well-composed alternative, has users, or reduces intervention/cost without correctness regressions. Neither a dashboard nor a successful optimizer demonstration proves that thesis.

A proposed unique capability is falsified when an existing solution already meets the accepted obligation with realistic integration cost. No named competitor is rejected in this pass, and no sunk investment confers a right to survive unchanged. A resulting product could be a narrow integration, maintained framework or reusable method; architecture and packaging await evidence.

Next research passes: full GEPA/library methods and alternatives, modern agent evaluation/security, experiment validity and dataset leakage, component/topology search methods, mature commercial/self-hosted comparison stacks, provenance/retention and exact version/license/project health.
