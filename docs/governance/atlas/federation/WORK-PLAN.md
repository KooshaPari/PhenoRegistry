## Scheduling

Requirements grouping is not a claim of final work sizing. These packets are bounded discovery/contract/witness stages; owners decompose implementation into actual claimable changes. Hard prerequisites encode needed artifacts, not preferred order. Native test devices, shared manifest edits and final review have separate resource constraints. Product delivery elsewhere must not wait for the whole federation horizon.
