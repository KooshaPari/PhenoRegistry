# Evidence-bound numeric product grading

Source: INT-006. This is a proposed implementation contract, not a live engine state.

## PROOF-16-01 — Literal score
Compute a numerical score and letter grade against a versioned applicable rubric.

Acceptance: The output names scope/profile/revision and never presents the result as universal quality.

## PROOF-16-02 — Frozen weights
Fix category/criterion weights and eligible units before assessing the candidate.

Acceptance: Adding many easy tests or duplicate criteria cannot silently dilute a failed critical criterion.

## PROOF-16-03 — Evidence admission
Bind earned units to exact candidate/profile/oracle identity, freshness and required channels.

Acceptance: Stale, wrong-build or no-pixel evidence earns no credit for a visible current feature.

## PROOF-16-04 — Unknown remains denominator
Missing, blocked, unrun and inadmissible obligations remain visible and earn zero.

Acceptance: Deleting missing outcomes from the evidence set cannot increase the grade.

## PROOF-16-05 — Noncompensatory gates
Keep critical invariants and required independent coverage gates outside averaging.

Acceptance: A high numeric grade with a failed critical invariant remains BLOCKED.

## PROOF-16-06 — Uncertainty truth
Distinguish assessed weight, unknown bound, stochastic intervals and evaluator class.

Acceptance: Model confidence is not accepted as a probability or proof.

## PROOF-16-07 — Scope-aware change
Publish same-scope progress and rubric/denominator changes separately.

Acceptance: A scope expansion is not misreported as a code regression or hidden to keep a score rising.

## PROOF-16-08 — Independent trust
Test graders and native adapters with adversarial controls and independent review.

Acceptance: A structurally valid invented receipt never claims authenticated product qualification.

## PROOF-16-09 — Role-specific profiles
Use appropriate GUI/game/library/CLI/infra and white/gray/black-box applicability.

Acceptance: Do not assert line coverage of inaccessible third-party engine internals.

## PROOF-16-10 — No rival scorecard
Integrate with existing accepted rubric/process; preserve unresolved SROC/CDP names.

Acceptance: No invented acronym or new registry becomes authority from this amendment.
